This is an internal copilot built for customer support at LMNTO. It answers the
questions that used to escalate to product or engineering: how a feature is
implemented, what its PRD specifies, and why an issue is happening for a
specific user.

Under the hood it's a **LangGraph** graph served by **FastAPI**: query
understanding and a **planner/router** hand each question to four specialist
agents — a **RAG agent** with hybrid, self-grading retrieval over implementation
docs and PRDs; a **text-to-SQL agent** whose queries are parsed, policy-checked
and EXPLAINed before they run read-only; a **knowledge-base agent** behind a
two-tier **Redis** cache; and a **tool agent** — and a response synthesizer
composes one cited answer. Every run is traced in **LangSmith**.

The linked case study walks through each agent's pipeline, the fallback routing
and the guardrails that make it safe to put in front of support.
