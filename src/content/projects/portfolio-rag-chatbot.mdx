This is the assistant in the corner of every page on this site. Ask it about my
experience, projects or writing and it answers from the portfolio's own content
— or tells you it doesn't know. It's deliberately small, deliberately
framework-free, and runs at **$0/month**.

The backend is a **FastAPI** service on Hugging Face Spaces with hand-rolled
two-stage retrieval: **NumPy** cosine recall, a **FlashRank** cross-encoder
rerank and a score floor that refuses without calling the model. Answers stream
over **Server-Sent Events**, follow-ups are rewritten into standalone queries,
and a model fallback chain keeps it answering when the free quota runs out. The
site reaches it through a same-origin **Cloudflare Worker** proxy.

The source is public on GitHub, and the linked case study walks through the
retrieval design, the refusal path, the free-tier engineering and the abuse
protection.
