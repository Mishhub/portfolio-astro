Reward and bonus payouts at LMNTO run through a three-stage pipeline: an
**immutable, versioned snapshot** of the inputs, **payout intents** derived from
it, and **settlement**. Pinning every run to a snapshot is what makes it
auditable, resumable after a failure and recomputable when inputs change — at
100+ payouts a second.

Settlement posts into a **double-entry ledger**, which commits each transaction
together with an outbox record and relays it to **Kafka** for other services.
Underneath, the ledger runs on **PostgreSQL sharded with Citus**, partitioned by
month, replicated to a read replica and backed up with **Barman PITR** — with the
restore actually tested.

The linked case study walks through the snapshot model, the ledger and its
outbox, and how the data layer scales and recovers.
